6 Practical Conversation Rules for Family & Caregivers

1. Reduce Background Noise: Turn off TV/radio and face your loved one directly within 3–6 feet.
2. Establish the Topic First: Ask them to announce the single-word topic (e.g., “Dinner” or “Medicine”) before speaking a full sentence.
3. Encourage “Think Loud, Speak Slow”: Remind them to take a full breath before starting each phrase.
4. Confirm Word-by-Word: Repeat back the words you understood so they only need to clarify the unclear word.
5. Respect Cognition: Never speak down to them—dysarthria is purely muscle movement, never intellect.
6. Watch for Vocal Fatigue: Schedule important conversations earlier in the day or after medication peaks.
